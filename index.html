Basics & Antiderivatives (Indefinite Integrals)

Integration is the reverse process of differentiation. If $\frac{d}{dx}[F(x)] = f(x)$, then $\int f(x)\,dx = F(x) + C$, where $C$ is the arbitrary constant of integration representing a family of parallel curves.

Key Takeaway Formula
$$\int x^n \, dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C \quad (n \neq -1)$$
